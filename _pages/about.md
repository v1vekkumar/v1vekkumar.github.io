### Director, Sound Research (Frontier AI) @ Google DeepMind
I lead a distributed team focused on pushing the boundaries of multimodal understanding and generation across audio, speech, and music. Our core research includes audio-visual editing, source separation, spatial audio, sound understanding, and working with foundation models.

Prior to joining Google in 2022, I spent over 13 years at **Dolby Laboratories**, where I founded and led the **Audio AI Research** team. There we developed state-of-the-art [speech compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.03021) and created novel [voice conversion](https://www.isca-speech.org/archive/Interspeech_2018/abstracts/1121.html) techniques by combining our Deep Learning expertise, audio experience, and understanding of human perception. My earlier work at Dolby included leading the development of core codecs ([Dolby Digital Plus](https://www.dolby.com/us/en/technologies/dolby-digital-plus.html) & [Dolby TrueHD](https://www.dolby.com/us/en/technologies/dolby-truehd.html) and enabling immersive audio experiences via [Dolby Atmos](https://www.dolby.com/us/en/technologies/home/dolby-atmos.html).
